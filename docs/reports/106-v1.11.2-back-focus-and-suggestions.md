## Before and after

Previously the quick generator offered only one Back area, the pinned source contained usable back movements absent from the extended library, and workout summary always linked to an opaque proposal list. That list displayed raw exercise IDs and did not explain the actual sets or the future saved-program default.

The quick generator now offers Full back, Upper back and Lower back. The historical `back` ID continues to mean Full back for existing saved plans and contextual ratings. Upper back matches primary middle-back/lats/traps; Lower back matches primary lower-back only, not secondary involvement. Focused Lower back is restricted to 15–30 minutes, with 35–60 minute choices disabled and Full back available for longer workouts. No warm-up or recovery assumptions were changed.

After a completed workout, the summary shows a suggestion count only when that session produced proposals. A directly generated one-off session records workout history but does not produce proposals; the page now explains that distinction. The proposals screen explains the logged working sets, recorded last set, saved target, reason, and optional **next** program default. The user may accept the suggested load, enter a different nonnegative load, decide later, or dismiss. Later/dismiss do not mutate a prescription. Acceptance changes only the saved program default; historical sets remain untouched. A non-load hold can be acknowledged without a load change.

## Source review and curation

The project's pinned Free Exercise DB snapshot (`b0eed061e1c8`) has 873 records. Of 27 source records whose primary muscle is lower back, the reviewed catalogue previously contained three. Two distinct additions were retained: **Superman** (controlled floor extension; browse-only mobility) and **Weighted Ball Hyperextension** (loaded ball-supported extension; `other` apparatus/ball station). Heavy partial deadlifts and good-morning variations, stretches and specialist setups were not imported merely to raise the count.

For the dos au sens large, 87 pinned-source records primarily target middle back, lats or traps. Thirty-nine were already retained; 48 were not. Three provide a distinct useful station/position without duplicating canonical pulls: **Lying T-Bar Row** (chest-supported machine), **Seated One-arm Cable Pulley Rows** (seated unilateral cable), and **Shotgun Row** (standing split-stance low cable). Kipping muscle-ups/rope climbs, behind-neck pulldowns, foam-roll stretches and close grip duplicates remain excluded. The five additions use ten locally committed start/end photos from the same pinned source. The reviewed catalogue has 307 records, 297 visible records and 277 generator-eligible records; 614 local media assets pass audit. This is selective expansion, not an 873-record import.

Source/technique references: [pinned Free Exercise DB repository](https://github.com/yuhonas/free-exercise-db), [ACE exercise library grouping](https://www.acefitness.org/resources/everyone/exercise-library/). ACE content or media was not copied. The existing source licence/attribution strategy is unchanged.

## Verification and limitations

- Targeted unit tests cover focus matching, 15/20/25/30-minute lower-back duration bounds, full/upper-back long sessions, catalogue eligibility/media, proposal session filtering, detail joins, and acceptance/later/dismiss history safety.
- TypeScript and package lint passed; classification audit reported 307 records, 18 explicit overrides and zero invariants; media audit reported 614 local assets.
- A full local Vitest run passed after limiting its worker count to two, avoiding unrelated test timeouts under parallel local contention. The first high-worker run had eight timeouts, not deterministic domain failures.
- Mobile generator screenshots at 360 and 412 CSS px show no horizontal overflow and the 30-minute Lower back cap. The local Playwright runner generated both screenshots but stalled during teardown, so its final local result is **not** claimed as passing; CI browser checks remain required.
- The source's anatomical tags and images are curated but this is not medical or biomechanical validation. Short focused low-back sessions are not a pain-management recommendation.
- Existing proposal rules can suggest a fixed increment from the saved prescription target; the user can dismiss or edit it. This change explains that behavior but does not implement individualized performance coaching or timed-prescription mutation.
