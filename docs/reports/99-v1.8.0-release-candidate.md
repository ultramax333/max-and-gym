# Max & Gym v1.8.0 release candidate

## Scope

- Quick-session exercises grouped and labelled by equipment.
- Full local photo and technique preview for every exercise in the active plan.
- Two-to-five working-set planning instead of a fixed three-set default.
- Contextual 1–5 ratings by exercise, body area and training goal.
- Transactional current-set expansion by trading one untouched future working set.
- Saved generated sessions retain their training context and equipment snapshots.

## Data safety

- No database migration or reset.
- Ratings use the existing backed-up `appMeta` table.
- Set-plan changes are atomic and idempotent.
- A donor exercise must be untouched and retain at least two working sets.
- Completed, started, warm-up and drop sets cannot be donated.
