## General training references used for conservative rules

- American College of Sports Medicine resistance-training guidance/position statements.
- Peer-reviewed reviews on resistance-training volume, load, frequency, proximity to failure, and warm-up.
- World Health Organization guidance on chronic primary low-back pain for avoiding medical overclaim.

The application does not present these as individualized medical advice.
