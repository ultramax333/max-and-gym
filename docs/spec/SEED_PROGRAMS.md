# Seed programs

These are initial content requirements and test fixtures. The user can edit every value. `RIR` means repetitions in reserve.

Session duration estimates include warm-up and transitions. Loads are user-entered; no absolute starting loads are prescribed.

## 1. Low-back-aware warm-up — 6 minutes

Use before 40/60-minute sessions when enabled.

1. Easy bike or treadmill walk — 2 minutes.
2. Gentle cat-camel — 6 controlled repetitions.
3. Bird dog — 5 controlled repetitions per side.
4. Glute bridge — 8 controlled repetitions.
5. Hinge-to-wall practice or unloaded session-specific movement — 8 repetitions.
6. Continue directly into specific ramp sets for the first lift.

Every movement can be disabled. The sequence is mobility/preparation, not rehabilitation.

## 2. Core 10 — Stability, minimal transitions

**Target:** 10 minutes. Two rounds. Move slowly between positions.

| Exercise | Prescription | Rest/transition |
|---|---|---:|
| Dead bug | 8–10/side, controlled | 20 s |
| Half-kneeling cable or band Pallof press | 10/side | 20 s |
| Side plank | 25–35 s/side | 20 s |
| Bird dog | 8/side, controlled | 30 s before next round |

Use a floor-only alternative for Pallof press when no cable/band is available. No rapid get-up.

## 3. Core 15 — Gym core and carries

**Target:** 15 minutes. Two to three rounds depending on pace.

| Exercise | Prescription | Rest/transition |
|---|---|---:|
| Cable Pallof press | 10–12/side | 20 s |
| Captain’s-chair knee raise or controlled reverse crunch | 8–15 | 30 s |
| Suitcase carry | 25–40 m/side | 30 s |
| Side plank or cable side hold | 25–40 s/side | 20 s |
| Dead bug with cable pulldown | 8–10/side | 45 s before next round |

All floor transitions are deliberate, not timed. Offer substitutions if any movement provokes symptoms.

## 4. Two-day program — 60-minute versions

### Full Body A — Squat and bench emphasis

1. Warm-up and primary ramp sets — 7 minutes.
2. High-bar back squat, hack squat, or user-locked equivalent — 4 × 4–6 at 2 RIR, 180 s rest.
3. Barbell bench press — 4 × 4–6 at 2 RIR, 180 s rest.
4. Chest-supported row — 3 × 6–10 at 2 RIR, 120 s rest.
5. Hip thrust or seated leg curl — 3 × 8–12 at 2 RIR, 90 s rest.
6. Cable lateral raise + rope pressdown superset — 2–3 × 10–15, 60 s between rounds.
7. Stationary-bike intervals — 6 minutes, low impact.

### Full Body B — Hip extension and vertical pull emphasis

1. Warm-up and primary ramp sets — 7 minutes.
2. Trap-bar deadlift, machine hip extension, or user-locked equivalent — 3–4 × 3–5 at 2 RIR, 180–240 s rest.
3. Standing or seated overhead press — 4 × 4–6 at 2 RIR, 150–180 s rest.
4. Pull-up/assisted pull-up or pulldown — 4 × 5–8 at 1–2 RIR, 120 s rest.
5. Bulgarian split squat or leg press — 3 × 8–12/side, 120 s rest.
6. Incline dumbbell curl + rear-delt cable fly — 2–3 × 10–15, 60 s between rounds.
7. Sled push/drag or incline walk — 6 minutes.

The hinge choice is not automatically treated as safe or unsafe. It follows user preference, history, and symptom notes.

## 5. Two-day program — 40-minute versions

### Full Body A — 40

1. Warm-up/ramp — 5 minutes.
2. Squat or hack squat — 3 × 4–6, 150–180 s.
3. Bench press — 3 × 4–6, 150–180 s.
4. Chest-supported row — 3 × 6–10, 90–120 s.
5. Hip thrust or leg curl — 2 × 8–12, 75–90 s.
6. Bike — 4 minutes if time remains.

### Full Body B — 40

1. Warm-up/ramp — 5 minutes.
2. Trap-bar deadlift or selected alternative — 3 × 3–5, 180 s.
3. Overhead press — 3 × 4–6, 150 s.
4. Pulldown/pull-up — 3 × 6–10, 90–120 s.
5. Leg press or split squat — 2 × 8–12, 90 s.
6. Sled/incline walk — 4 minutes if time remains.

## 6. Three-day program — 60-minute versions

### Full Body A — Strength: squat + horizontal press

1. Warm-up/ramp — 7 minutes.
2. Squat pattern — 4 × 3–6 at 2 RIR.
3. Bench press — 4 × 3–6 at 2 RIR.
4. Chest-supported row — 4 × 6–10.
5. Seated leg curl — 3 × 8–12.
6. Lateral raise + triceps superset — 3 × 10–15.
7. Bike — 6 minutes.

### Full Body B — Strength: hip extension + vertical work

1. Warm-up/ramp — 7 minutes.
2. Trap-bar deadlift, Romanian deadlift, hip thrust, or machine alternative — 3–4 × 3–6 at 2 RIR.
3. Overhead press — 4 × 4–8.
4. Pull-up/pulldown — 4 × 5–10.
5. Bulgarian split squat or leg press — 3 × 8–12.
6. Curl + rear-delt superset — 3 × 10–15.
7. Sled or incline walk — 6 minutes.

### Full Body C — Hypertrophy + conditioning

1. Warm-up — 6 minutes.
2. Leg press or pendulum squat — 4 × 8–12.
3. Incline dumbbell press — 4 × 6–10.
4. Seated cable row — 4 × 8–12.
5. Hip thrust or machine glute press — 3 × 8–12.
6. Cable fly + lateral raise + arm movement tri-set — 2–3 rounds of 10–15.
7. Low-impact bike or sled intervals — 8 minutes.

## 7. Three-day program — 40-minute versions

Reduce each day to:

- 5-minute warm-up/ramp;
- two primary movements at 3 working sets;
- one pull or lower-body secondary movement at 3 sets;
- one 2-set accessory or superset;
- 4–5 minutes optional low-impact conditioning.

The engine creates explicit exercise lists from the 60-minute templates rather than merely hiding unfinished items.

## 8. Controlled variation pools

Examples:

- Squat anchor: high-bar squat, safety-bar squat, hack squat, pendulum squat.
- Horizontal press: barbell bench, paused bench, dumbbell bench, machine chest press.
- Supported row: chest-supported T-bar, machine row, incline-bench dumbbell row, cable row.
- Hip extension: trap-bar deadlift, Romanian deadlift, hip thrust, machine hip extension.
- Vertical pull: pull-up, assisted pull-up, neutral-grip pulldown, single-arm pulldown.
- Conditioning: bike, incline walk, elliptical, sled.

Main-lift selection remains stable within the block unless the user replaces it.

## 9. Progression defaults

- Primary lifts: smallest available load increase after all prescribed work is achieved at target RIR; hold after one miss; propose reduction after two comparable misses.
- Hypertrophy lifts: double progression across the repetition range.
- Accessories: double progression with controlled rotation.
- Conditioning: increase one variable at a time.
- All changes are proposals requiring approval.
